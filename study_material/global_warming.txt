Global Warming

Global warming is the long-term increase in Earth's average temperature.

The main cause of modern global warming is the increase of greenhouse gases
in the atmosphere. Human activities such as burning coal, oil, and gas
release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.

Other greenhouse gases include methane and nitrous oxide.

Global warming can cause melting ice, rising sea levels, changes in weather
patterns, and harm to ecosystems.

Reducing greenhouse gas emissions can help limit global warming.
